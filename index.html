How it works

Deliverables

What you receive, and our one ask.

Six concrete deliverables for your company. In return, we only ask for a certificate of completion.

Deliverables you receive

  1. Project charterProblem, scope, target and savings baseline, signed off by your sponsor.
  2. Root-cause analysis reportData analysis with statistical evidence of what is really causing the problem.
  3. Implemented solutionPiloted with your team and verified on the process, not just recommended.
  4. Savings validation sheetAnnualised ₹ savings, calculated and agreed with your finance or plant team.
  5. Control plan & SOPsSPC charts, updated SOPs or checklists, and a response plan for your process owner.
  6. Final report & management presentationA complete write-up and a closing presentation to your leadership, with handover.

Certificate of completion

When the project closes, we request a certificate of completion from your company, on your letterhead, confirming the project title, duration and team members.
